h1. Walmart Seller Center — Action Inventory & Risk Classification for AI Automation

{info:title=About this document}
This page catalogs the seller-facing actions in Walmart Seller Center and classifies each one by risk for autonomous execution by *Sage*, the new seller-assistant AI agent. Use it to define Sage's autonomy scope, opt-in defaults, and human-in-the-loop policies.

*Source*: Compiled from a walkthrough of [Marketplace Learn|https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides] (the authoritative Seller Center documentation portal, whose IA mirrors the in-app left navigation), supplemented with product knowledge. Owner partner context: *100009*. Live UI was not entered (SSO + MFA blocked autonomous login — see appendix).
{info}

h2. Risk rubric

|| Tier || When it applies || Default policy for Sage ||
| {status:colour=Green|title=LOW|subtle=false} | Reversible in one click; no / minor customer-facing impact; < $50 equivalent financial impact | *Autonomous* — Sage acts, then logs the action |
| {status:colour=Yellow|title=MEDIUM|subtle=false} | Reversible with moderate effort; some customer-facing impact; $50–$5,000 financial impact | *Opt-in* — Sage acts only if the seller has enabled the category in preferences (with bounds) |
| {status:colour=Red|title=HIGH|subtle=false} | Irreversible / cascading; significant brand, customer, legal, or account-level impact; > $5,000 financial impact | *Human-in-the-loop* — Sage drafts and requests explicit approval; never executes alone |

{tip}When in doubt, escalate one tier. A misclassified LOW that should have been MEDIUM erodes trust faster than the inverse.{tip}

h2. Autonomy policy recommendations for Sage

h3. {status:colour=Green|title=LOW — Autonomous (default ON)}

These 17 actions are the bedrock of Sage's value: they are reversible, customer-invisible (or trivially so), and free. Sage should execute them on its own initiative, log the action with reasoning, and surface a daily digest. Examples:

* Fill missing optional attributes from the seller's own data library
* Refresh repricer / search insight views
* Claim expiring ad credits
* Mark internal to-dos complete; dismiss stale alerts
* Generate / schedule reports

*Guardrails*: daily action-count cap, allowlist of action types in {{sage.policy.low}}, and a one-click "undo last batch" from the Sage console.

h3. {status:colour=Yellow|title=MEDIUM — Opt-in with bounds (default OFF, easily enabled)}

These 28 actions are where Sage delivers leverage but must respect seller-set rules. They become Autonomous only after the seller opts in *per category* and configures bounds:

* *Price changes* — opt-in toggle + floor/ceiling % and absolute $ bounds per SKU
* *Bulk content edits* — opt-in toggle + max items per run + mandatory dry-run preview
* *Repricer assignments* — opt-in toggle + allowed-strategy list
* *Ad budget adjustments* — opt-in toggle + ±% cap and absolute daily ceiling
* *Promotional enrollments* (non-event) — opt-in toggle + max discount % and duration cap
* *Listing Quality / Assortment Growth accepts* — opt-in toggle + diff-preview required

*Guardrails*: every MEDIUM action emits an in-app notification + email, has a 24-hour "rollback" button, and is summarized in a weekly Sage activity report. If 3 sellers/customers complain inside a window, Sage auto-suspends that category and notifies the seller.

h3. {status:colour=Red|title=HIGH — Human-in-the-loop (always)}

These 23 actions are *never* executed by Sage alone, even if the seller wants to opt in. Sage may *draft* the action (e.g., compose a return-decision recommendation, pre-populate a POA, propose a deletion list) and *queue it for approval* in the Sage console, but the seller must click *Approve* in the live Seller Center session. Categories include:

* Account, identity, payout, and tax actions (1099-K, payout method, ACH, business info, brand registry, user roles, API key issuance)
* Order-lifecycle commitments (ship-confirm, cancel, change ship date)
* Refunds, return approvals, customer concessions
* Performance disputes, policy appeals, Plan-of-Action submissions
* High-impact catalog operations (delete items, change product IDs, manage duplicates with merges)
* Event-locked or large-discount pricing (Walmart Deals, clearance, Automate Pricing T&C acceptance)
* Large new advertising commitments (new campaigns, new shipping methods)

*Guardrails*: pending HIGH-risk drafts expire after 72h, are signed with Sage's reasoning, and require fresh MFA on the seller side to execute. Every HIGH execution is double-logged in Sage's audit trail and the Seller Center activity log.
